Two Google Meet settings to turn off

Both are on by default, and both work against a drum lesson. Takes ten seconds.

  1. Noise cancellation, off. Meet treats a drum as background noise and ducks it, so I cannot hear what you are actually playing. Settings → Audio → Noise cancellation
  2. Framing, off. Auto-framing keeps re-centring on your face and crops your hands out of the shot. Settings → Video → Backgrounds and effects → Appearance → Framing
