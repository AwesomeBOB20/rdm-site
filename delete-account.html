Delete your account

You can delete your RDM account yourself, from inside the app, in about a minute. This page also covers requesting deletion by email if you can no longer sign in.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Sign in at app.rudimentaldrummingmastery.com or open the RDM app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. If you have an active subscription, cancel it first with Manage subscription.
  4. Under Delete account, press Delete…, type DELETE to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your account and everything stored with it: your profile and login, practice history and streaks, badges, favorites, playlists, saved presets, uploaded sheet music, weekly log photos and videos, and your community posts. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What we keep

Payment records tied to past purchases are kept by our payment processor (Stripe) as required for tax and accounting. They are not linked to a usable account once the account is gone.

Can't sign in?

Email anthony@rudimentaldrummingmastery.com from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We verify the request came from the account's own email and complete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.
